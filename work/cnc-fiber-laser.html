The difference

We cut our own parts

Most fab shops send plate work out and wait. We run a CNC fiber laser, a rotary tube chuck and CNC plasma in our own Odessa shop — so your brackets, gussets, base plates and custom components get cut the same week they're drawn.

See it running

The machine at work

The rotary chuck takes tube and pipe up to 14 in. and cuts it to length, notches it, or puts holes in it without a single layout mark or torch cut.

The flat bed runs plate to 2 in. on the 12 kW fiber source, and our CNC plasma picks up anything heavier. Nothing leaves the yard to get cut.
