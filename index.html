The difference

We cut our own parts

Most fab shops send plate work out and wait. We run a CNC fiber laser, a rotary tube chuck and CNC plasma in our own Odessa shop.

8 ft × 20 ftLaser bed
12 kWFiber source
2 in.Max plate, laser
14 in.Tube & pipe chuck

The rotary chuck takes tube and pipe up to 14 in. and cuts it to length, notches it, or puts holes in it without a single layout mark or torch cut. The flat bed runs plate to 2 in. on the 12 kW fiber source.

Your brackets, gussets, base plates and custom components get cut the same week they're drawn — no third-party queue between design and weld-out.
