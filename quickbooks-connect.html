Connect QuickBooks

This page connects the State of the Arc crew portal to QuickBooks Online, so that approved weekly work can be turned into invoices without re-typing it.

What the connection does

  • Reads your customer list and your products and services, so a job can be matched to the right customer.
  • Creates invoices from work that an administrator has already reviewed and approved.
  • Never sends an invoice to a customer. Invoices are created unsent, for review inside QuickBooks.

What it does not do

  • No access to payroll, banking or payments data.
  • No access to any company file other than our own.
  • No financial data is copied out of QuickBooks. We store only record identifiers.

Connecting

Connecting is done by an administrator using a private link issued by the office. If you administer this system and need that link, contact the office on (432) 248-1455.

Already connected?

Reconnecting is only needed if the connection has expired or was revoked. Running the connect step again replaces the stored connection; it does not create a second one. To end the connection, see Disconnect QuickBooks.
