The difference

We Cut Our Own Parts

8 ft × 20 ftLaser bed 12 kWFiber source 2 in.Max plate, laser 14 in.Tube chuck 4 in.+CNC plasma

Most fab shops send plate work out and wait. We run a CNC fiber laser, a rotary tube chuck and CNC plasma in-house, which means your brackets, gussets, base plates and custom components get cut the same week they are drawn.

That control shows up three ways on your job:

  • Schedule. No third-party queue between design and weld-out.
  • Fit-up. Laser-cut parts drop together, so less grinding and less rework in the field.
  • Change orders. A revised part gets re-cut in hours, not next week.
