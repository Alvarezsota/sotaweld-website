{{#}}. Shop Rules

The short version. Your supervisor will go over anything specific to a job site.

Attendance & hours

  • Be on time and ready to work at the start of your shift.
  • Late or absent? Call your supervisor — don't just text — at least 1 hour before your start time.
  • Missing 2 scheduled days in a row without calling is treated as quitting.
  • Record all your hours accurately, the same day. No working off the clock, and never record time for someone else.
  • Get overtime approved ahead of time. Overtime you work is always paid.

Safety

  • Wear your PPE at all times in the shop and in the field (Section {{ref:ppe}}).
  • Follow hot work permits, fire watch and lockout/tagout rules on every job.
  • Never weld or cut on a drum, tank or line that held anything flammable until it has been cleaned and tested.
  • Follow every customer's site rules and orientation.
  • Stop and report anything unsafe. You won't be punished for it.

Phones

  • No phone use while welding, grinding, cutting, running equipment or driving.

Company vehicles & fuel cards

  • Drive only with a valid license and Company approval.
  • No personal use unless approved, and no passengers who aren't on Company business.
  • Seat belts on. No handheld phone while driving.
  • Report any accident or ticket right away.
  • Fuel cards are for Company vehicles and equipment only.

Tools & property

  • Company tools stay Company property. Sign them out and return them at the end of the job, and when you leave the Company.
  • Don't take material, scrap or equipment without permission.
  • Report broken or damaged tools and equipment.

Conduct

  • No drugs, alcohol or working impaired (Section {{ref:ua}}).
  • No weapons in the shop, Company vehicles or on customer sites, except as Texas law allows in your locked personal vehicle.
  • No harassment, threats or fighting. Treat coworkers and customers with respect.
  • Smoke only in designated areas, away from gas cylinders and anything flammable.

Breaking these rules can lead to discipline, up to and including termination. These rules don't change the at-will relationship.

{{#}}. Injury Reporting

Report every injury — even small ones — the same shift.
  1. Emergency? Call 911 first. Then call your supervisor.
  2. Tell your supervisor right away and before the end of your shift. This includes cuts, burns, arc flash / "welder's flash," metal in the eye, strains and near misses.
  3. Get care. Use the first aid kit and eyewash for minor injuries. For anything more, the Company will direct you to the clinic listed below.
  4. Fill out the Injury & Incident Report (Section {{ref:incident}}) with your supervisor within 24 hours.
  5. Post-accident drug and alcohol test may be required (Section {{ref:ua}}).

Why same-shift reporting matters

Texas law gives you 30 days to report a work injury, but late reports make it harder to get treatment covered and to fix the hazard. That's why Company policy is the same shift.

No retaliation. The Company will not punish you for reporting an injury, filing a workers' compensation claim or raising a safety concern. Texas law and federal OSHA protect these rights.
