How these numbers get checked

Every weld report is matched against the field reports coming off the job. The two have to agree before anything gets billed, so what you turn in is what the customer sees.

Nobody is trying to catch anyone out. Miscount a joint, fat-finger a number, forget a line — tell the office and it gets put right, no more said. That happens and it is fine.

Adding welds you did not run is a different thing altogether. It puts a bill in front of a customer for work nobody did, and it is grounds for dismissal. Log what you actually welded and this will never once be your problem.

Inches only. What each man welded, which job it was on and what it billed stay on his own report.
